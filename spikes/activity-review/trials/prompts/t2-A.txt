# Feature: a discount on the stock's value

## Goal
`Stock.total_value(discount)`: the stock's value less `discount` percent. The discount is a percentage from 0 to 100; anything else is refused with `ValueError`. Without a discount, the value is as today.

## Out of scope
- The report's format (`Stock.report`).
